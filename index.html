PERFORMANCE INFRASTRUCTURE FOR HOSPITALITY

Why Influ-Pay Wins

Revenue-Aligned Creator Marketing — Built for Verified Outcomes

Influ-Pay transforms influencer marketing into a structured, fraud-resistant, and economically aligned performance engine where restaurants pay only for validated dining outcomes — not impressions, not reach, not speculation.

01

Seated Covers, Not Vanity Metrics

We redefine the unit of value. Restaurants don’t earn from likes — they earn from seated guests. Influ-Pay anchors payouts to verified seated covers and attended bookings, aligning marketing spend directly with operational revenue.

02

Dual-Key Attendance Proof

Each conversion is verified using venue confirmation combined with customer-side ephemeral proof. This creates tamper-evident receipts that dramatically reduce fraud, replay abuse, and attribution disputes.

03

Capacity-Aware Pricing

Influ-Pay introduces yield-managed CPA. Restaurants can pay more for hard-to-fill time slots and less for peak demand, transforming influencer marketing into a dynamic revenue optimisation tool.

04

Settlement Finality Built In

Every conversion follows a defined lifecycle: Pending → Verified → Settled. Clear dispute windows and clawback logic replace vague negotiations with deterministic payout rules.

05

Compliance by Design

Disclosure controls, audit logs, and automated compliance linting prevent risky claims before publication. Compliance is embedded as infrastructure — not left to manual oversight.

06

Fraud-Resistant Architecture

Replay prevention, velocity monitoring, anomaly clustering, and venue baseline modelling protect both creators and restaurants, keeping marketplace integrity high.

Everything You Need to Know About Influ-Pay

Clear answers on how Influ-Pay delivers measurable performance, secure validation, and ROI-driven collaboration between restaurants and creators.

What makes Influ-Pay different from traditional influencer marketing? +
Influ-Pay operates on verified outcomes, not impressions. Restaurants only pay for confirmed seated customers validated through secure digital tracking and receipt tokenisation.
How is customer attendance verified? +
Attendance is confirmed through a dual-key validation system that merges venue confirmation with a secure digital token, ensuring accurate attribution and eliminating fraud.
Do creators receive guaranteed payments? +
Creators are compensated based on verified performance outcomes. This aligns incentives with real customer conversions and ensures fair, transparent settlement.
How does Influ-Pay protect restaurants from fake conversions? +
Receipt tokenisation and digital proof layers prevent manipulation. Payments are released only after validation thresholds are met.
Can campaigns be customised? +
Yes. Restaurants can define payout per seated cover, budget caps, timing controls, and capacity rules tailored to operational needs.
What type of creators can join Influ-Pay? +
Verified local creators with authentic audience engagement and location-based relevance are eligible to activate campaigns.
Is Influ-Pay scalable across multiple venues? +
The platform is designed for multi-location deployment, enabling centralised performance tracking and consistent ROI measurement.
How quickly are settlements processed? +
Settlements are automated and released once performance validation is complete, ensuring predictable and secure financial flow.
