About Influ-Pay

Influ-Pay is a creator marketing platform that helps restaurants and local businesses collaborate with creators to promote their products and services. The platform connects brands with relevant creators to increase visibility and reach new audiences through authentic digital content.

Businesses can discover creators, manage marketing campaigns and measure campaign performance through a simple platform designed for modern creator-driven marketing strategies.

Influ-Pay focuses on building structured partnerships between creators and brands, enabling measurable campaign performance and long-term marketing growth.

Our Vision

To simplify creator marketing for restaurants and local brands by providing a platform that connects businesses with creators and helps campaigns deliver measurable results.

Founder

Influ-Pay was founded by Rushabh with the vision of helping restaurants and local brands grow through creator-driven marketing. The platform was built to simplify collaboration between businesses and creators while ensuring campaigns deliver measurable marketing impact.

By combining creator discovery, campaign management and performance insights, Influ-Pay enables brands to build authentic partnerships with creators and reach new audiences through engaging digital content.
